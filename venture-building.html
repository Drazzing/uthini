The journey

How venture building works

A clear, professional path from first conversation to launch and beyond.

  1. Share your imagination Submit your business idea and tell us why you’re the right person to lead it.
  2. Evaluation & fit We review the opportunity together — assessing vision, feasibility, market potential, and partnership alignment.
  3. Partnership agreement Equity split, roles, and responsibilities are defined upfront with clear expectations on both sides.
  4. We deliver Uthini executes across software development, Australian company registration, legal foundations (NDAs, shareholder agreements, software licences, distribution agreements), branding, and initial marketing launch.
  5. Launch & grow together You run the company daily. We support with strategic guidance, product iterations, and scaling advice through our management seat.
